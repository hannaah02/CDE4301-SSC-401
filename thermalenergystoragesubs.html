Thermal Energy Storage

Thermal Energy Storage (TES) of our modular CSP system includes:

  • TES structures
  • Heat transfer enhancements
  • Phase change materials (PCMs)

The design specifications are summarised below.

Thermal Energy Storage Structures

Existing TES systems

Among the existing TES systems, the water tank with PCMs shows the best overall suitability due to its strong compatibility with CSP systems, good commercial availability, and moderate cost and complexity. In contrast, solid thermocline, TCES calcium looping, and two-tank molten salt systems are more complex and less practical for small- or medium-scale applications. Overall, the water PCM hybrid system offers a more efficient and feasible solution for compact CSP-based thermal storage.

Existing TES structures

Latent heat storage containers are generally classified as compact or encapsulated. Compact systems hold the PCM in a large vessel with an internal heat exchanger, often of the shell-and-tube type, commonly used in water-heating applications. Encapsulated systems contain the PCM in many small sealed units over which the heat-transfer fluid flows, offering greater design flexibility and suitability for both air and water heating networks. A void space is included in each unit to allow for PCM expansion during melting.
